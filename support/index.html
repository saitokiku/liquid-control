Support

How can we help?

Answers to the common questions, and a way to tell us about a problem.

Questions

Windows doesn’t list my phone

At first, Windows 11 lists only common kinds of device. Open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Devices → Bluetooth devices discovery → Advanced, then add the device again.

Which entry in the Bluetooth list is my phone?

Your iPhone shows up as “Liquid Control” and a four-character code of its own, like “Liquid Control 3F2A”.

How do I use it with a Mac?

Pair them over Bluetooth, like a PC: there’s nothing to install on the Mac. On iPhone, tap +, then Mac; then on the Mac open System Settings → Bluetooth and connect your phone.

How do I pair a Stick?

Hold its button for 3 seconds, then add it in the iPhone app. The Stick isn’t sold yet: get notified.

Is there an Android app?

It’s coming soon, for Android 12 or newer: on Google Play, and as a download from Get it.

What stays private?

Liquid Control has no account, no ads and no analytics. What you type and say goes only to the device you’re controlling. The privacy policy has the details.

Report a problem

Tell us what happened, on which phone or watch, with which device, and the app version. Reports are GitHub issues, and issues are public: don’t include passwords, emails or anything personal.
