Get Liquid Control

Free, in public beta. Every download and link is here: start with your phone, then add your devices from the app.

Public beta

iPhone and Apple Watch

The public beta is on TestFlight, Apple’s app for betas. It needs iOS 17 or later, and the Apple Watch app comes with it. The App Store comes soon.

  1. On your iPhone, tap Join the beta. If you don’t have TestFlight yet, the page takes you to it first.
  2. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Open Liquid Control and add your first device.

Android

Liquid Control for Android works as a Bluetooth keyboard and trackpad for computers, tablets and TVs, over Bluetooth Classic or LE. It needs Android 12 or newer.

Not in this beta yet: voice, TVs over Wi-Fi, the Stick, and the link to Liquid Control for Mac.

Download the APK

SHA-256: published with the release

Install it

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. Google Play Protect may warn that the app comes from an unknown developer, because the beta isn’t on Google Play yet. You can still choose to install it.
  4. Open Liquid Control, then pair your phone from the Bluetooth settings of your computer, tablet or TV.

Update it

Download the new APK and install it over this one.

Works now

Mac

Your Mac pairs with your phone over Bluetooth, like a PC, as a keyboard and trackpad. There’s nothing to install.

  1. On iPhone, open Liquid Control and tap +, then Mac. On Android, open Liquid Control.
  2. On the Mac, open System Settings → Bluetooth and connect your phone. An iPhone shows up as “Liquid Control” and a four-character code; an Android phone, by its own name.

Liquid Control for Mac: coming soon.

Nothing to install

Windows, Linux, ChromeOS, iPad and TVs

Computers, tablets and TVs that take a Bluetooth keyboard pair with your phone over Bluetooth: add the device in Liquid Control, then choose your phone in the device’s Bluetooth settings.

With the iPhone app, Google TV, Android TV, Roku and LG webOS (beta) TVs are found on your Wi-Fi instead: tap +, then Smart TV.
