Get Liquid Control

The apps are free, in public beta: get the one for your phone, then add your devices from the app. The Stick is on pre-order, and its delivery depends on FCC equipment authorization.

Public beta · Free

iPhone and Apple Watch

The public beta is on TestFlight, Apple’s app for betas. It needs iOS 17 or later; the Apple Watch app comes with it and needs watchOS 10 or later. The App Store comes soon.

  1. On your iPhone, tap Join the beta. If you don’t have TestFlight yet, the page takes you to it first.
  2. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Open Liquid Control and add your first device.
Join the beta

It’s a beta, so expect rough edges. TestFlight lists the app as WristPad, its working name, for now. New betas show up in TestFlight, which can install them for you. To tell us what broke, take a screenshot and send it from TestFlight, or report a problem.

Public beta · Free

Android

The public beta is a download, for Android 12 or newer running 64-bit Android on ARM (arm64-v8a). It won’t install on a phone that runs 32-bit Android, as some low-cost phones do even with a 64-bit chip, or on an x86 one. Google Play comes later.

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, let your browser install apps (Install unknown apps), then tap Install. Play Protect may say it doesn’t know the developer: tap More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Open Liquid Control and add your first device.

Pre-order

The Liquid Control Stick

A small USB receiver for computers that can’t pair over Bluetooth: it types what your phone sends as a USB keyboard and mouse. It ships in February 2027.

Pre-orders and the FCC: The Stick is subject to the FCC’s rules, and delivery depends on it completing FCC equipment authorization: nothing ships before then. FCC rules don’t address consumer protection, contract or other federal or state law, which still apply to your order. If authorization isn’t granted, every pre-order is refunded in full, and you can cancel yours for a full refund any time before it ships.

Works now

Mac

Your Mac pairs with your phone over Bluetooth, like a PC, as a keyboard and trackpad. There’s nothing to install.

  1. On your iPhone, open Liquid Control and tap +, then Mac.
  2. On the Mac, open System Settings → Bluetooth and connect your iPhone. It shows up as “Liquid Control” and a four-character code.

Nothing to install

Windows, Linux, ChromeOS, iPad and TVs

Computers, tablets and TVs that take a Bluetooth keyboard pair with your phone over Bluetooth: add the device in Liquid Control, then choose your phone in the device’s Bluetooth settings.

With the iPhone or Android app, Google TV, Android TV, Roku, LG webOS and Samsung TVs are found on your Wi-Fi instead: tap +, then Smart TV.
